# Plan: a channel's name after "Configure relays", a server test's error, hiding a profile, leaving the QR scanner, the SimpleX Lock notice

These cases record what the app shows, for a reviewer who reads the screenshots of runs before and after a change. Take every shot the plan names, when the plan says, and say in the case's `record` note what the shot shows. Run the cases in this order, and go on after a case that shows a bug. Before each part, the chat list shows, unless the case says otherwise.

| ID | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|

| C2 | `ui do click-text "Configure relays" 10 -- wait "Network & servers" 20 -- shot C2-network`. | "Network & servers" opens. |
| C3 | `ui key BACK` until the form "Create public channel" shows (not further), then `ui shot C3-back`. Say what the name field shows, and read it from the picture too. Then back to the chat list. | The name field still shows `ci name`. Record bug if it is empty. |
