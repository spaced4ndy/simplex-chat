# Plan: the chat list banner's close button in the dark theme

The chat list shows one banner at a time: "Invest in SimpleX Chat!" ("Crowdfunding on Wefunder"), or "Support SimpleX" ("Get badge + better files") once the profile has more than 3 chats. A banner shows its close button, an "X" (`multiply` in the tree) at its top right, only after the banner was tapped once. Only one banner shows per app start: after a banner has shown, another one appears only after `ui relaunch`.

These cases capture how the X looks, in the dark and the light theme, for a reviewer who compares the screenshots of runs before and after a change. Take every shot the plan names, exactly when it says, with nothing covering the banner (no keyboard, no sheet, no alert). For each banner shot also run `ui find multiply` and quote its line: it gives the X's position for the reviewer.

| ID | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Open the picker (your profile image, `Image "Account"`), "Settings", "Appearance". Tap the "Color mode" picker and choose "Dark". Close Settings (swipe it down, or its close button). `ui shot B1-dark`. | The app is drawn dark: a black background, light text. |
| B2 | The profile has no conversations yet, so the chat list shows the onboarding pages ("Talk to someone"). If "Invest in SimpleX Chat!" shows below them, tap it; a sheet about investing opens: take `ui shot B2-invest-sheet`, then close the sheet by swiping it down. If the banner is not offered, record B2 blocked with "not offered" and go on. | The sheet opened and closed; the onboarding pages show again. |
| B3 | Create a group: tap "Compose" (the pencil), "Create group", tap the field by its value "Enter group name…", `ui type g1`, check the field's value in the tree, tap "Create group", then "Skip" on the next screen. Go back to the chat list. If B2 tapped the banner, the chat list now shows "Invest in SimpleX Chat!" with its X: `ui find multiply`, `ui shot B3-invest-dark`. If B2 was blocked, take the shot anyway. | The chat list shows "g1". When the Invest banner shows, its X is at its top right and stands out clearly from the banner behind it. Say in the record what colours you see for the X and for the banner right around it. |
| B4 | Create two more groups the same way, "g2" and "g3", and go back to the chat list. Run `ui relaunch` and wait for the chat list. "Support SimpleX" shows in the chat list (once the app has loaded the profile's badge state, which can take a few seconds: wait up to 30 s with `ui wait "Support SimpleX" 30`). Tap the banner; a sheet about supporter badges opens: take `ui shot B4-support-sheet`, then close it by swiping it down. The banner now shows its X: `ui find multiply`, `ui shot B4-support-dark`. | The list shows g1, g2 and g3. The "Support SimpleX" banner shows its X at its top right, and the X stands out clearly from the banner behind it. Say what colours you see for the X and for the banner around it. |
| B5 | Open Settings → "Appearance" → "Color mode" → "Light", close Settings, wait for the chat list: `ui find multiply`, `ui shot B5-support-light`. Then set "Color mode" back to "Dark", close Settings, `ui shot B5-support-dark-again`. | In the light theme the X shows grey on the light banner. Back in the dark theme the banner and its X look as in B4. |

Record a case ok when its shots are taken and its Expect holds, and bug when the X does not stand out from the banner behind it, with the colours you saw; the reviewer decides from the screenshots in either case.
