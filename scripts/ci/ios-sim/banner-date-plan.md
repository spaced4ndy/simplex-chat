# Plan: the chat banner when a contact request changes the open chat, and chat row dates

These cases capture what the app shows, for a reviewer who compares the screenshots. Take every shot the plan names, exactly when it says, with nothing covering the part it shows (no keyboard, no alert unless the case says so). In each `record` note quote what you saw: the labels and values from the tree. Record a case ok when its shots are taken and its Expect holds, and bug when its Expect does not hold, with what you saw; the reviewer decides from the screenshots in either case. Do not run a case recorded bug a second time: go on to the next case.

`<app>` below is the app's profile name as a peer shows it in `peer <name> chats` or `peer <name> members '#<group>'`: the smoke test typed `citester`, and the keyboard may have changed it. Look it up before the first peer command that needs it.

## How to do things

- **Connect the app to a peer's link** (a 1-time link, an address or a group link): `ui open-link '<the link>'`. An alert offers "Open new chat" (or "Open group"): tap it. The chat opens, not yet connected, with a bar at the bottom ("Connect", "Join group"): tap it. Go back to the chat list afterwards with the back button at the top left of the chat.
- **The chat banner** is the card with the chat's name and picture at the top of the chat's messages. Its last line says what the chat is, for example "Accept contact request" or "Your contact". `ui find` reads it; if the chat has many items, scroll up to it with `ui swipe 200 300 200 700`. Quote both the name on the banner and its last line.
- **The app's address:** open the picker (your profile image in the toolbar, `Image "Account"`), tap "Create SimpleX address"; on the screen that opens, tap "Create SimpleX address" when it is offered, and wait for the address (a QR code and "Share address"). If an alert asks whether to share the address with contacts, decline it. Tap "Share address", in the share sheet tap "Copy" (`ui find Copy`; if it isn't in the tree, `ui tap-xy` at its position in the screenshot), and close the sheet. `ui clipboard` prints the link: this is `<address>`. Close the address screen by swiping it down. Don't change the address settings: requests to it are not accepted automatically.
- **A contact request** to the app's address shows in the chat list as a row with the requester's name and "Open to accept". Open it by tapping the row: the chat shows the banner, and a bar at the bottom with "Reject" and "Accept".
- **A group's members:** in the group's chat tap the group's name at the top (the element with the smallest y from `ui find <group>`, with `ui tap-xy`); the group's info opens with its members listed. Tap a member to open the member's info; its buttons under the name include "message". Close sheets by swiping them down.
- **A row's actions:** swipe the row to the left (`ui swipe 370 <y> 60 <y>`): it offers actions such as "Delete" and "Clear", or "More" with them. Confirm in the alert that follows.

## A contact request changes the open chat

The address is used only in R1 and R3; reqa, reqb, hostr and memb are used only in their cases.

| ID | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Create the app's address. `peer reqa connect '<address>'`, `peer reqa wait 10`. On the chat list: `ui shot R1-list`. Open the row "reqa": `ui shot R1-request`, `ui find "Accept contact request"`. Tap "Accept" in the bottom bar (if an alert asks how, tap "Accept"). At once: `ui shot R1-accepted-now`. Then `peer reqa wait 30`; `ui shot R1-accepted`, `ui find "Your contact"`, `ui find "Accept contact request"`. If the bottom bar still shows "Accept", run `peer reqa wait 30` once more and take `ui shot R1-accepted-later`. Go back to the chat list, open the chat with reqa again: `ui shot R1-reopened`. Go back to the chat list. | R1-request: the banner shows the name reqa and "Accept contact request". After Accept, without reopening (R1-accepted, or R1-accepted-later if taken): the banner shows the name reqa and "Your contact", and the message field replaces the bar. R1-reopened shows the same banner. |
| R3 | `peer reqb connect '<address>'`, `peer reqb wait 10`. Open the row "reqb": `ui shot R3-request`. Stay in this chat for the rest of the case. `peer reqb profile reqbee`, then `peer reqb connect '<address>'` again (the peer sends its request again, now as reqbee), `peer reqb wait 10`. Without leaving the chat: `ui shot R3-updated-now`, `ui find reqbee`, `ui find reqb`. `peer reqb wait 5`, `ui shot R3-updated-later`. Go back to the chat list: `ui shot R3-list`. Open the chat again: `ui shot R3-reopened`. Go back to the chat list. Don't accept or reject this request. | R3-request: the banner shows the name reqb and "Accept contact request". R3-updated-now and R3-updated-later, without reopening: the banner shows the name reqbee and "Accept contact request", and the bar with "Reject" and "Accept" stays. R3-list and R3-reopened show reqbee. |
| R2 | `peer hostr group mgrp`, `peer hostr group-link mgrp`: it prints the link. Connect the app to the link ("Open group", then "Join group"), `peer hostr wait 30`. `peer memb connect '<the same link>'`, `peer hostr wait 30`, `peer memb wait 30`. Repeat `peer hostr wait 30` and `peer memb wait 30`, at most 3 times, until `peer memb members '#mgrp'` lists `<app>` as connected. In the app open "mgrp", its group info, tap the member memb, and tap "message" in memb's info. The chat with memb opens, with "Send direct message to connect" at the bottom: `ui shot R2-before`. Stay in this chat. `peer memb member-contact '#mgrp' '<app>' 'hello from memb'`, `peer memb wait 10`. Without leaving the chat: `ui shot R2-request-now`, `ui find "Accept contact request"`, `ui find "hello from memb"`. `peer memb wait 5`, `ui shot R2-request-later`. Tap "Accept" in the bottom bar, `peer memb wait 30`: `ui shot R2-accepted`, `ui find "Your contact"`, `ui find "Accept contact request"`. If the bottom bar still shows "Accept", run `peer memb wait 30` once more and take `ui shot R2-accepted-later`. Go back to the chat list, open the chat with memb again: `ui shot R2-reopened`. Go back to the chat list. | R2-before: the banner shows the name memb and "Your contact". R2-request-now and R2-request-later, without reopening: the banner shows memb and "Accept contact request", the bottom bar shows "Reject" and "Accept", and "hello from memb" is in the chat. After Accept, without reopening: the banner shows memb and "Your contact", and the message field replaces the bar. R2-reopened shows the same banner. |

## Chat row dates

|---|---|---|
| D1 | Connect the app to `peer alice link`, then `peer alice wait 30`; connect the app to `peer bob link`, then `peer bob wait 30` (once more each if the chat doesn't show the contact as connected). `peer bob send '@<app>' 'bob one'`, `peer bob wait 10`, then `peer alice send '@<app>' 'alice one'`, `peer alice wait 10`. Go back to the chat list: `ui shot D1-list`. | Every row shows today's time. |
| D2 | `peer alice group club`, `peer alice invite '#club' '@<app>'`, `peer alice wait 20`. On the chat list, opening nothing: `ui shot D2-invited-live`. | The row "club" shows today's time, not 01/01/70. |
