# Plan: member info of a member who left, opened from their earlier message

Two peers, alice and bob, are in the group `club` with the app's profile (citester), which is an admin there. A member's profile picture is the round image left of their message, under their name: clicking it opens the member's info in the right pane ("Member" section, the member's role, and actions such as "Block for all", "Remove member" or "Delete member messages"; a connected member also shows a "Servers" section with "Receiving via"). The info can be longer than the pane: scroll it with `ui scroll <x> <y> down` over the pane to see its end. Press Escape to close it and get back to the chat.

These cases record what the member info shows before and after bob leaves, for a reviewer who compares the screenshots of runs before and after a change. Take every shot the plan names, exactly when it says, and in the record list the rows and buttons the info shows, by their text.
