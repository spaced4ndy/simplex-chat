# Plan: About opened from Settings, and "Allow" with default contact preferences

These cases capture what the app shows, for a reviewer who compares the screenshots of runs before and after a change. Take every shot the plan names, exactly when it says, and in each `record` note quote the texts you saw. Record a case ok when its shots are taken and its Expect holds, and bug when it does not, with what you saw; the reviewer decides from the screenshots in either case. Do not run a case recorded bug a second time, even when `record` suggests it: go on to the next case.

- **Connect the app to a link a peer printed:** `ui clipboard '<the link>'`, then the pencil (about 363,730) → "Paste link" → "Tap to paste link". The app shows what the link is for: click "Open new chat" if offered; the chat opens with a "Connect" button at the bottom: click it, then `peer <name> wait 30`.
- **Contact info:** click the contact's name at the top of the right pane. It shows the buttons "search", "call", "video" and "mute" under the name. "Contact preferences" is a row further down; scroll with `ui scroll <x> <y> down` over the pane if it is below the window. Press Escape to go back.
- **Contact preferences:** each feature has a section with the feature's name as its header and a row "You allow" showing the value, for example "default (yes)", "yes", "always" or "no".
- **Chat preferences of the profile:** click the profile picture (about 27,730), then "Chat preferences". Each feature has a row with its name and a value; clicking the value opens a menu. "Save (and notify contacts)" is at the end of the list. Press Escape to go back.
